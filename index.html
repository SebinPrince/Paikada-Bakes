Our Story

Crafting Sweet Memories in Kottayam

At Paikada Bakes, we believe that every celebration deserves a centerpiece that is as delicious as it is beautiful. Located in Melukavumattom, Kottayam, we specialize in high-quality, customized, oven-fresh cakes baked from the comfort of our home kitchen.

We take pride in using only the premium ingredients—free from preservatives and artificial texturizers—ensuring every bite tastes absolute perfection.
